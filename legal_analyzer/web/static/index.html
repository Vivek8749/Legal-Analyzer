    Find the fine print that works against you

    Upload a contract, terms of service, lease, or employment agreement. The analyzer retrieves the clauses that matter, has an AI model review them for one-sided terms, loopholes, and waived rights, and explains each one in plain English, with the exact quote and page.

    Use a frontier model (Claude, GPT, Gemini) or run a local model with Ollama so the document never leaves your computer. Pick the model in the header.

    1. UploadThe document is split into clauses and indexed locally for keyword and semantic search.
    2. AnalyzeEach risk area (liability, termination, data, disputes, fees, and more) is reviewed in parallel against the most relevant clauses.
    3. UnderstandRead prioritised findings, check every quote against the source, and ask follow-up questions with cited answers.

    Try it with samples/sample_terms_of_service.pdf, a deliberately one-sided document for a fictional company.
